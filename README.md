  # AutismCare AI

## 1. Project Overview

AutismCare AI is an AI-powered application designed to support parents and caregivers of children with autism. The application was inspired by seeing a parent in my community struggle to understand some of the challenges associated with caring for a child with autism.

The goal of the application is to make autism-related information easier to understand and provide parents with practical ideas, tools to organise their concerns, and support with daily routines.

AutismCare AI is designed for educational and supportive purposes. It does not diagnose autism or replace advice from qualified healthcare, developmental, educational, or therapeutic professionals.

## 2. Features

The application includes the following features:

* **AI Chatbot:** Provides general educational information and practical suggestions about behaviour, communication, routines, and sensory needs.
* **Signs & Concerns:** Helps parents record observations and understand that these observations may have different explanations. It does not diagnose autism.
* **My Child's Concerns:** Organises a parent's concern into a summary, possible factors, practical suggestions, things to monitor, and topics to discuss with a professional.
* **AI Task Planner:** Generates flexible daily routines that parents can edit to suit their child's needs.
* **AI Research Assistant:** Provides simplified summaries, key points, practical information, and questions to discuss with professionals.
* **Saved Information:** Allows users to keep useful AI responses, concerns, research, and plans.
* **Dashboard Layout:** Provides a central dashboard with access to the application's main features.
* **Sidebar Navigation:** Helps users navigate between different sections of the application.
* **Responsive Design:** Designed to work across desktop, tablet, and mobile screen sizes.
* **Editable AI Outputs:** Allows users to edit generated information where the feature supports editing.
* **Responsible AI Disclaimer:** Explains the application's limitations and encourages professional guidance when appropriate.

## 5. Responsible AI and Safety

AutismCare AI provides general educational and supportive information. It does not diagnose autism and does not replace professional assessment or advice. AI-generated information may contain errors, so parents and caregivers should consult qualified professionals when making important decisions about a child's health, development, education, or wellbeing.

## 6. Project Goal

The goal of AutismCare AI is to make autism-related information more accessible and understandable, help parents organise their concerns, and provide practical starting points for everyday support while promoting the responsible use of artificial intelligence.
